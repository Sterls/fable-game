Privacy Policy

Common Ground · Effective November 13, 2026

The short version

  • There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.
  • You choose a nickname. Your answers, drawings and prompts are shared only with the people in your game room.
  • Game rooms aren't stored on our servers. Game messages are relayed live and are gone when the room closes.
  • If your host saves results to a crew leaderboard, your nickname and game stats are stored so your group can compare them over time.
  • We never sell or share your information for advertising.

We don't collect your real name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos or advertising identifiers.

Service providers

Game messages and crew stats go through Supabase, which hosts our real-time relay and database. Like any web service, our hosting providers may briefly process technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the app and keep it secure. They do this on our behalf, not for their own purposes.

Who can see what

  • During a game: everyone in the room sees your nickname, answers, drawings and likes once each round is revealed. Custom prompts stay hidden until they come up.
  • Crew leaderboards: anyone who has the crew's code can view its leaderboard, which shows nicknames and game stats. Saving results to a crew requires the crew PIN.
  • Please don't put personal or sensitive information in your nickname, answers, drawings or prompts.

How long we keep it

  • Game rooms: not stored. Messages exist only while they're being delivered.
  • Crew stats: kept until the crew or its data is deleted on request.
  • On your device: kept until you clear the app's data or your browser's site data.

Your choices

  • You can play without any crew stats being saved. The host decides whether a game is saved, and can turn it off for any game.
  • To have your stats removed from a crew leaderboard, or a whole crew deleted, contact us (see below) with the crew code and the nickname used.
  • You can clear everything stored on your device by clearing the app's storage or your browser's site data.

Children

Common Ground isn't directed at children under 13, and some optional rounds include mildly mature humor. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has used a crew leaderboard, contact us and we'll delete the data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Questions or deletion requests: contact the developer at the email address listed on Common Ground's Google Play store page.
