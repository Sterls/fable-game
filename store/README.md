Answer in secret, match your friends, and laugh at the reveal. No accounts.

**Full description**

Common Ground is a calm party game for friends who are together in person or on a call. Everyone answers the same prompt on their own phone, in secret. Then the answers are revealed, and you score a point for every friend who said the same thing.

It's easy to start: one person creates a room and everyone else joins with a 4-letter code. There are no accounts, no ads and no timers unless you want them.

MIX OF ROUNDS
• Word rounds: "A pizza topping", "Something you do on a rainy day"… can you think alike?
• Sketch rounds: draw your answer. Everyone guesses who drew what before the reveal.
• Quick picks: four options, one tap.
• Spicy would-you-rathers: side with the majority to score.
• Spotlight rounds: everyone tries to match one player.

MADE FOR FRIENDS
• Like your favorite answer. The most liked gets a bonus point.
• Add your own prompts. Inside jokes welcome, and they stay secret until they come up.
• The host can merge answers that mean the same thing ("NYC" and "New York"). Feel free to argue your case.
• Round-by-round recap at the end, drawings included.
• Optional crew leaderboard: track wins, who's most in sync, and which pair of friends thinks most alike.

CALM BY DESIGN
• Soft colors, no sound, no flashing. Dark mode supported.
• Turn-based, so a slow connection never costs you a round.
• Up to 12 players.

**Category:** Games → Party (or Word / Casual)

**Tags to consider:** party, friends, multiplayer, word, drawing
